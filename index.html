Privacy Policy

Key Medical Aesthetics operates an automated messaging and scheduling assistant across our official Facebook Page and Instagram Professional account to respond to patient inquiries, assist with appointment scheduling, and analyze our clinic's advertising performance.

Information We Collect:

  • Platform identifiers (Facebook Page-Scoped User ID or Instagram User ID) and public profile name.
  • Messages, inquiry details, and preferred appointment dates/times sent voluntarily to our inbox.
  • Aggregated, non-personal ad performance and page engagement metrics via Meta APIs.

How We Use & Protect Your Information:

  • Used strictly to answer service inquiries, coordinate clinic consultations, and improve patient support.
  • We never sell, rent, or share patient messages or personal details with third-party marketers.
  • Sensitive medical records and clinical photos are handled directly by authorized clinic staff and are not stored in public logs.

User Data Deletion Instructions

You may request the deletion of your messaging history and inquiry records stored by Key Medical Aesthetics at any time:

  1. Message our official Facebook Page (Key Medical Aesthetics) or Instagram account with the phrase "DATA DELETION REQUEST" along with your full name.
  2. (Optional) To revoke app permissions from your Facebook account, go to Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites, select our app, and click Remove.
  3. Our team will permanently delete your stored conversation logs and booking inquiry records within 7 business days and send a confirmation reply.
